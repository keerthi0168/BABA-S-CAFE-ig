Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How much notice do you need for a custom cake? +

For simple designs, same-day or next-day is usually possible. For elaborate tiered or wedding cakes, 2–3 days' notice helps us plan better.

Do you deliver, or is it pickup only? +

Message us on WhatsApp with your location — we can confirm if delivery is possible for your area, or you can collect from the shop.

Can I send a reference photo for the design? +

Yes — send it directly on WhatsApp along with the message you'd like written on the cake.

What payment methods do you accept? +

Cash and UPI are accepted at the shop. Confirm payment details with us directly when placing a larger order.
